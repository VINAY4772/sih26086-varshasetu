📖 Data Provenance, Model Validation & Scientific Methodology

Department & Architecture: National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF) and IMD operational criteria. Hybrid Physics-Informed Random Forest Classifier coupled with tropical climate drivers (ENSO Niño 3.4, BOM IOD, CPC MJO).

Evaluation Status: Evaluated using chronological train/test partitioning (Train: 2023–2024, Test: 2025) to prevent temporal data leakage. Demonstrated positive Brier Skill Score (+0.158) over climatological baseline.

📊 System Data Provenance & Operational Classification
  • Real External Telemetry: NOAA CPC ONI (real monthly Oceanic Niño Index SST anomalies) & NASA POWER API agro-climatology (precipitation, temperature, humidity, surface wind, insolation).
  • Historical Real Catchment Data: Multi-year observed agricultural weather datasets (Tenali, AP 2023–2025) validated using chronological train/test partitioning without temporal leakage.
  • Climatological Benchmark Fallback: 850 hPa zonal westerly wind and satellite OLR utilize climatological benchmark distributions when live NCMRWF ERPS upper-air operational feeds are unavailable.
  • Model-Derived Probabilities: Hyperlocal onset probability, active spell duration, break spell risk & duration, and heavy-rainfall probability computed via VarshaSetu physics-informed Random Forest classifier.
  • Synthetic Demonstration Datasets: Pre-configured scenario profiles across representative mandals/blocks (Dharmasagar, Gangavathi, etc.) for interactive prototype simulation.
