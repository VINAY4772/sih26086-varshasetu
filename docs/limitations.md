# Known Limitations & Scientific Assumptions: SIH26086

## 1. Meteorological & Physical Limitations
1. **Convective Scale Variability:** Pre-monsoon and monsoonal convective cells can produce high localized variability over a 5 km grid. While the model evaluates block/village catchments, localized shower intensity may differ between adjacent farm plots.
2. **Horizon Decay:** Predictability beyond 14 days is governed by subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) atmospheric chaos. Horizons of 21 and 30 days should be interpreted as probabilistic trends driven by ENSO/IOD/MJO rather than daily rainfall forecasts.
3. **Data Access Dependencies:** Official IMD real-time gridded data updates annually or requires formal departmental credentials. The prototype implements a verified synthetic demonstration benchmark for local testing and demonstration.

---

## 2. Agronomic Assumptions & Ethical Guidance
1. **Soil Micro-topography:** Advisories assume typical drainage and soil depths for the agro-climatic zone. Low-lying plots subject to inundation or shallow stony soils require field-specific adjustments.
2. **Local Agricultural Consultation:** This system does not replace the official advisories issued by State Agriculture Departments, Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK), or Mandal Agricultural Officers (MAO). Farmers should consult extension officers before undertaking irreversible investments.
3. **Pesticide / Herbicide Spraying:** All chemical recommendations (such as foliar Potassium Nitrate KNO3) are general agronomic guidelines and must comply with local approved safety data sheets and dosage standards.
